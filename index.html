Common questions

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone. Tread has no accounts and doesn't collect data. Deleting the app deletes its data; workouts saved to Apple Health stay in Health.

Why aren't my workouts showing up? Open Settings > Health > Data Access & Devices > Tread and make sure Workouts is turned on.

Why are form metrics labelled as estimates? They're measured by your iPhone's motion sensors and get steadier on longer runs, especially with the phone in a pocket or armband.
