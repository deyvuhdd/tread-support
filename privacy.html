Tread Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Tread ("the app") is made by David Laurent. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

The short version

Tread does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything you add or record stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your shoes, shoe photos and notes
  • Runs, walks, hikes and court sessions you record or log, including GPS routes, pace, splits, steps and form metrics
  • Your settings and preferences

This data is stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes it.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Tread reads workouts, routes, distance, steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO₂ max, active energy, body weight and your age from Apple Health. It uses them to match workouts to your shoes, estimate calories and running metrics, and show how your fitness is trending. Tread can also save the activities you record back to Apple Health, with their route, splits, heart rate, running metrics, effort and weather. Health data is never sent off your device, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone.

Location and motion

Tread uses your location only while you are recording an activity, to measure your route and distance. It uses your iPhone's motion sensors and barometer to count steps, measure climb, and estimate cadence and running form. This data stays on your device.

Weather

To show the weather during a run and adjust estimates for heat and cold, Tread asks Apple Weather for the conditions at the run's start time and an approximate location, rounded to about 1 km. Your route is never sent. Apple Weather does not receive your name or any account information.

Bluetooth

If you use a Bluetooth heart-rate strap, or WHOOP in heart-rate broadcast mode, Tread connects to it only while you record an activity, to show your live heart rate. Readings stay on your device and in Apple Health.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you take a run selfie or a photo of a shoe. Photos are saved on your device, or shared only when you choose to share them.

Shoe images

When you browse the shoe catalog or add a shoe from a product page, Tread loads product images from the shoe brand's website. Those requests contain no personal information about you.

Children

Tread is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
